Finite element course projects

The projects below come from courses in finite element analysis and structural design.

Morphing wing: finite element analysis

A 3D solid Abaqus model of a 4D-printed composite morphing wing, built from 27 parts to capture thickness changes, layup sequence and the corrugated core, with tie and contact interactions under pulling and pushing loads. Its deflections followed the experimental trends across load cases, and the model was used to study stress in the full wing and in individual plies.

Compaction roller: design by simulation

A concave polyurethane compaction roller for automated fiber placement, designed so that it presses harder and longer near the tape edges. An Abaqus model, driven by Python scripts, swept the roller geometry, and the manufactured roller matched the simulated pressure patterns in pressure-film tests.

Corrugated-core sandwich panels

Abaqus models of 3D-printed sandwich panels with triangular, trapezoidal, rectangular and circular cores, under flatwise compression and three-point bending, correlated with physical tests and then used to study wall thickness, corrugation angle and load location.

Thermal design of a PCB heating plate

A printed circuit board plate for a genetic-testing application, designed to hold two copper electrodes at different, nearly uniform temperatures on an FR4 board. The geometry was refined over several optimization stages in Ansys Workbench.

Lightweight bracket optimization

An ABS-PC load-bearing bracket designed in SolidWorks and analysed in Abaqus over ten iterations, removing low-stress material and easing stress concentrations around the loading area and support holes to raise strength for its weight.

Stiffened composite fuselage section

A finite element model in MSC Apex/Nastran of a fuselage section with composite skin, metallic stiffeners and defined laminate layups, used to find where stress and deflection peak under load.
