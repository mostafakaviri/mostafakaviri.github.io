Autonomous smart wheelchair

Navigation and motion control for an electric wheelchair at a robotics startup.

The problem

The wheelchair drives indoors, in enclosed spaces where GNSS is not available, so it cannot rely on satellite positioning. It has to know where it is from its own sensors and a map, and then navigate smoothly: it carries a person, the load changes from one passenger to the next, and it moves among people and furniture.

What I did

  • Navigation stack built on ROS 2 and Nav2
  • GNSS-free localization: LiDAR map-based localization with AMCL
  • Global path planning on the map
  • EKF sensor fusion of wheel odometry and IMU for state estimation
  • Navigation treated as a control problem: MPPI controller tuning for tracking, smoothness and obstacle avoidance
  • Simulation scenarios for replanning, costmaps and behavior-tree recovery
  • Dynamics model identified from data recorded on the prototype

Results

The navigation pipeline runs end to end in simulation, and the identified dynamics model keeps the simulated chair close to the real one. This was industry work, so details stay private.
